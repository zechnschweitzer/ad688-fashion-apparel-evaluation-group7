Note on Sample Size

The final cleaned dataset contains 19 job postings. This reflects a known limitation of NAICS-based industry classification: many analytics roles at apparel/retail companies are tagged under unrelated NAICS codes rather than under clothing retail codes specifically. To compensate, this dataset combines NAICS 458 (Clothing, Clothing Accessories, Shoe, and Jewelry Retailers) with an exact-match list of known apparel/fashion company names, then filters to narrow analyst job titles (business analyst, data analyst). The resulting sample size is small but each row is a verified, legitimate match.
