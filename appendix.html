Opportunity Scorecard Reference

The full methodology is on Final Analytics. The scoring tiers behind each component are reproduced here for quick reference.

Table 12: Scoring tiers behind the Opportunity Score
Component Score Rule
Skill fit 0-4 +1 for each of the team’s four strongest self-rated skills (Data Analysis, Microsoft Excel, Stakeholder Management, Project Management) found in the posting’s listed skills
Accessibility 3 Minimum years of experience = 0
Accessibility 2 Minimum years of experience 1-2
Accessibility 1 Minimum years of experience 3-5
Accessibility 0 Minimum years of experience > 5
Employer signal 1-5 Raw count of postings from that employer in the 19-posting sample
