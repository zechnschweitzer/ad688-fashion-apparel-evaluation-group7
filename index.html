Project Overview

This project evaluates analytics-related career opportunities within the apparel and fashion industry using job-posting data from the MET Career Compass dataset.

The analysis focuses on a narrowly defined market built around NAICS 458 and a verified list of apparel and fashion employers. The current filtered dataset contains 19 job postings and is intended to provide a descriptive view of the selected market rather than represent the broader analytics labor market.

Key Findings

Our filtered dataset contains 19 verified business/data analyst postings in apparel and fashion retail (NAICS 458). Power BI and SQL are the two most requested skills, each appearing in 7 of the 19 postings. Vuori and TJX are the most active employers in the sample, each posting 5 of the 19 roles.

Our team rates itself strongest in Data Analysis and Microsoft Excel, and weakest in Power BI and Python, the opposite of what the market asks for most. The opportunity scorecard ranks Vuori’s Sr. Manager, Ecommerce Analytics role as the strongest match for our team right now.

See Market Baseline, Skill Gap Analysis, and Final Analytics for the full breakdown.
